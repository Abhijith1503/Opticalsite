Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you visit.

Do I need an appointment for an eye test?

No, walk-ins are welcome. Booking through the inquiry form keeps your waiting time short.

How long does it take to make my glasses?

Most single vision glasses are ready in 2 to 4 days. Progressive lenses can take up to a week.

Can I use my old prescription?

Yes, if it is less than a year old. We can retest your eyes if you are unsure.

Do you offer a warranty?

Frames and lenses carry a warranty against manufacturing defects. Ask in store for details.
